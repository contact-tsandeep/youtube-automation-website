# Privacy Policy

**Application:** Youtube-Automation  
**Contact:** contact.tsandeep@gmail.com  
**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026

## 1. Information Accessed
Youtube-Automation uses Google OAuth to access authorized YouTube channel information, video metadata, and analytics, and to upload and manage videos.

## 2. How Information Is Used
Information is used to automate video creation, publishing, scheduling, channel management, and performance analysis.

## 3. Data Storage and Third Parties
The application is self-hosted and stores operational data locally. AI-generated content workflows may transmit relevant information to configured third-party AI providers. YouTube information is accessed through Google's authorized APIs.

## 4. Data Protection
Access tokens and other credentials should be protected from unauthorized access. The application does not sell personal information.

## 5. User Control
Users can revoke Google account access through their Google Account security settings and request information about data deletion by contacting the support email.

## 6. Contact
For privacy questions, contact contact.tsandeep@gmail.com.
